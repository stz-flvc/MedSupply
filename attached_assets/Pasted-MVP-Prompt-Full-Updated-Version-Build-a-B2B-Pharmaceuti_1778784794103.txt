MVP Prompt — Full Updated Version
Build a B2B Pharmaceutical Distribution Management System.
Middleman platform between importers/suppliers and wholesalers, hospitals, and bulk buyers. No retail/walk-in functionality. Three distinct user types, each with their own signup, dashboard, and workflow.

1. BUYER
Signup:
Full registration form — full name, company name, company address, phone number, email, password, business type (wholesaler / hospital / pharmacy), CAC registration number or business license upload.
Account is pending until Admin verifies and approves.
Dashboard:

Product Marketplace — displays all admin-verified vendor products in a clean grid (Jumia/Jiji-style cards with white background product images, product name, vendor, price per unit)
Search bar — search by product name, category, or vendor
Product Detail Page — click a product → shows full details → input field to enter desired quantity → submit order request
Cart/Orders — view all submitted orders with current status (Pending / Confirmed / Proceed to Pay / Fulfilled)
Notifications — real-time status updates pushed by admin (e.g. "Your order has been confirmed, proceed to payment") — shows as popup and in notification bell
Payment — when admin triggers "Proceed to Pay", a popup appears on buyer's screen prompting payment confirmation → on confirm, redirects to Paystack checkout


2. VENDOR
Signup:
Full registration — company name, contact person, phone, email, password, NAFDAC license number, importer/distributor license upload, CAC document upload, product categories they deal in.
Account is pending until Admin verifies and approves.
Dashboard:

Upload Product — form to upload each product with:

Product name, category, description
Product image upload → background auto-removed and replaced with white (clean, professional display)
COA (Certificate of Analysis) upload
NAFDAC number / barcode / product license details
Quantity available
Price per unit


My Products — list of all uploaded products with status: Pending Verification / Verified / Rejected
Notifications — alert when admin verifies or rejects a product (e.g. "Your product Amoxicillin 500mg has been verified and is now live")
Vendor can upload multiple products, each going through the same upload and verification process


⚠️ Products not yet verified by admin are completely invisible to buyers. Only verified products appear on the marketplace.


3. ADMIN
Signup/Login:
Email and password only. Admin account is pre-created (not self-signup).
Dashboard:

User Verification Queue

List of pending buyers and vendors awaiting approval
View submitted details and uploaded documents
Approve or Reject with a reason
On approval, buyer/vendor gets notified and gains access to their dashboard


Product Verification Queue

List of products uploaded by vendors awaiting review
Admin reviews product details, COA, license, NAFDAC number
Approve → product goes live on buyer marketplace + vendor gets notified
Reject → vendor gets notified with reason


Order Management

View all active buyer orders
Update order status step by step:

Order Received
Order Confirmed
Proceed to Pay → triggers popup on buyer's screen → buyer confirms → redirected to Paystack
Payment Confirmed
Fulfilled/Delivered




Buyer & Vendor Management — full list of all registered users, ability to suspend or remove accounts
Notifications panel — log of all system activity


General Notes

Background removal on product images — auto-process on upload, output is clean white background
Paystack integration — payment only triggered by admin action, not buyer-initiated
All notifications — both in-app (bell icon) and popup where critical
Role-based access — each user type sees only their own dashboard, no crossover
UI style — clean, professional, minimal. Product marketplace feels like a B2B catalog not a retail store


Scope: MVP demo. Focus on complete UI flows, role logic, and the order/verification pipeline. Paystack can be sandbox mode for demo.
